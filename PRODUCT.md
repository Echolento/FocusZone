# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Someone who wants to do focused work and needs a barrier against their phone. They use FocusZone on their own phone, in a calm/quiet setting, to set a session length and put the device down.

## Product Purpose

A focus-timer app that gets you to start a session and look away from the screen. Success is a session started with minimal friction and a phone that stays face-down while work happens.

## Brand Personality

Calm, disciplined, quiet. Confident, understated, no gamification or noise.

## Anti-references

No cluttered gamified apps (badges, streaks, confetti, loud colors). No corporate dashboard SaaS feel (gray cards, tables, dense controls). No generic pastel timer apps.

## Design Principles

- Calm before energy: the interface should lower arousal, not hype it up.
- Restraint with a single confident accent: dark, minimal, one deliberate touch of color.
- Friction-free start: the path from open to session start is short and obvious.
- Quiet confidence: nothing flashes, nothing shouts, everything earns its place.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Reduced motion matters: animations get calm/instant fallbacks. Strong color contrast; never rely on green alone to communicate.
